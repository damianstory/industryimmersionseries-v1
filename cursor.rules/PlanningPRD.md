# Industry Immersion Series - Product Requirements Document

## Document Overview
This PRD outlines the requirements for the "Industry Immersion Series" website, a platform designed to connect Canadian students in grades 7-12 with industry-sponsored skill-building activities. This document focuses primarily on Milestone 1 (informational website), with notes on future milestones for context.

## Product Vision
The Industry Immersion Series is an educational initiative that partners with different companies and brands to create monthly skill-building activities for students. Each month features a different industry sponsor, providing students with opportunities to develop relevant skills while learning about career paths within those industries.

## Objectives
- Create awareness about diverse career opportunities across different industries
- Provide students with engaging, industry-relevant skill-building activities
- Offer financial incentives through microgrants to encourage participation
- Build connections between students, educators, and industry partners
- Establish a platform for ongoing educational engagement

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** Students in grades 7-12 across Canada
- **Secondary:** Educators and parents who can promote the program
- **Tertiary:** Industry partners/sponsors

## Milestone 1: Informational Website (Current Focus)

### User Stories
1. As a student, I want to learn about upcoming industry challenges so I can prepare to participate.
2. As an educator, I want to understand how the program works so I can share it with my students.
3. As a parent, I want to know about the incentives available so I can encourage my child to participate.
4. As a visitor, I want to sign up for updates so I can be notified when the program launches.

### Site Structure & Content Requirements

#### 1. Header Navigation
- Logo/Brand identity
- Navigation links that scroll to corresponding sections:
  - Home
  - Timeline
  - How It Works
  - Incentives
  - Why Micro Grants?
  - Contact/Sign Up
- Mobile-responsive menu for smaller screens

#### 2. Hero Section
- Compelling headline that clearly communicates the program concept
- Brief subheadline explaining the value proposition (skill building + career exploration + incentives)
- High-quality hero image that represents diverse students engaged in learning
- Pre-launch call-to-action form with:
  - Email input field
  - Role selection dropdown (Student, Educator, Parent)
  - Submit button with clear value proposition (e.g., "Get notified at launch")
  - Privacy statement/link

#### 3. Timeline Section
- Visual timeline representation of the program (October 2025 to June 2026)
- Each month displays:
  - Month name/date
  - Sponsoring company/industry
  - Brief description of that month's focus or theme
- Clear indication of which sponsors are confirmed vs. to be announced

#### 4. How It Works Section
- Step-by-step explanation of the program process:
  1. Monthly release of industry-sponsored challenges
  2. Students complete the activities
  3. Submission process (via Google Forms)
  4. Selection of winners and distribution of microgrants
- Simple visual icons/illustrations to accompany each step

#### 5. Incentives Section
- Clear breakdown of the different incentive structures:
  - **Monthly Incentives:** 20 x $500 microgrants per month
  - **Student Completion Incentive:** Prize for completing all eight monthly activities
  - **Educator Incentive:** Monthly prizes for educators on the mailing list
  - **School Incentive:** Prize for schools with 100+ student participants who complete all activities
- Visual elements to make the incentives engaging and clear

#### 6. Why We're Doing This Section
- Mission statement and purpose of the initiative
- Statistics or information about the importance of career exploration for students
- Brief explanation of how industry exposure benefits students' future career decisions
- Quotes or testimonials (if available from early partners or educational advisors)

#### 7. Footer
- Secondary CTA for email signup (duplicate of hero CTA)
- Contact information
- Social media links (if applicable)
- Copyright information
- Privacy policy link
- Terms of service link

### Technical Requirements (Milestone 1)
- Single-page website with scroll navigation
- Mobile-responsive design
- Email capture form with role selection
- Google Form integration capability (for future challenges)
- SEO optimization
- Accessibility compliance

### Non-Functional Requirements
- Page load time under 3 seconds
- 99% uptime
- Secure data handling for collected emails
- Compliance with Canadian privacy regulations
